How grades work

Grade Component Details
100% Final Examination The final exam consists of a combination of multiple-choice and essay questions. It is closed book, except for a single sheet of paper with your notes on both sides.
Adjustments While quizzes, practice essays, and discussion boards are not part of the final grade directly, grades may be adjusted downward for repeated failures to complete assignments, excessive unexcused absences, or lack of adequate preparation.

Policies

Attendance

Regular class attendance is required. To satisfy asynchronous attendance, students must view all videos and submit all knowledge checks, quizzes, boards, and assignments; failure to do so on time may result in an unexcused absence. Synchronous classes are on Thursdays on Zoom. Students are allowed two unexcused absences. Excessive unexcused absences may reduce your grade or deny permission to take the final examination.

Participation

Students will primarily be called from an alphabetical list, with random selections for hypotheticals. If you are unable to prepare because of illness or emergency, email by 4:30 p.m. on the day of class to be excused. On-the-spot "passes" are not permitted.

Online Learning

Refrain from multitasking during class. Inappropriate language or online harassment will not be tolerated. Please use your preferred name and pronouns in Zoom. Video is generally expected to be turned on. Use the "raise hand" feature to volunteer or ask questions, and reserve the chat for public, class-related messages only.

AI Policy

This course aligns with the Student Handbook's academic integrity policies. To discourage reliance on AI, points will be taken off from essay responses that deviate substantially from the material in the course (e.g., by discussing cases or doctrines not covered).

Title IX Policy

Sexual misconduct will NOT be tolerated. Faculty members are committed to a safe environment and must report any sexual misconduct to the Title IX Coordinator.
